it is NOT a shared public feed. Right now the bot's Quotex price stream doesn't

analysis engine to read the wrong trend direction and fire losing signals.

The Quotex WS connection is either (a) using a stale/expired session token that 
was never refreshed after the initial connection, or (b) opening on a generic/
unauthenticated handshake instead of one tied to the actual logged-in account 
session. Either way, the data received diverges from the real account's price 
path over time even while the connection shows "connected."
