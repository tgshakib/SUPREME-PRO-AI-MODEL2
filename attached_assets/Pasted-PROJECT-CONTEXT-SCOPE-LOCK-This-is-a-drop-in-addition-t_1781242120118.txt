WEEKDAY PLAYBOOK (select block via datetime.now().weekday(), Mon=0):

[MONDAY = 0 — RANGE DAY]
1. Build the day's range from the first 4-6 hours of candles (Asian + early session): range_high, range_low.
2. Treat range_high/range_low as live S/R.
3. Only fire when price taps range_high or range_low AND prints a rejection candle (wick >= 1.5x body, opposite-direction close).
4. SELL on range_high rejection, BUY on range_low rejection.
5. If a candle closes outside the range with body > 1.5x the 20-bar average body (expansion), suppress range signals and flag a possible early breakout for Tuesday's logic.

[TUESDAY = 1 — BREAKOUT DAY]
1. Pull Monday's stored range_high/range_low as the breakout reference.
2. Detect breakout candle: close beyond range_high/range_low with body size AND volume both above their 20-bar averages.
3. Confirm with either a successful retest (price returns to the broken level and holds) OR an immediate continuation candle in the same direction.
4. Fake-breakout filter: if price closes back inside the range within 1-2 candles of the "breakout," discard — no signal.
5. Confirmed signal = breakout direction (BUY on upside break, SELL on downside break).

[WEDNESDAY = 2 — TREND CONTINUATION DAY]
1. Determine prevailing trend from the last 20-30 candles using swing structure: HH+HL = uptrend, LH+LL = downtrend.
2. Wait for a pullback into a prior structure level / order block in the trend direction.
3. Require BOS confirmation: price must break the most recent swing high (uptrend) or swing low (downtrend) after the pullback.
4. Signal = continuation in trend direction once BOS confirms. No counter-trend signals on Wednesday.

[THURSDAY = 3 — REVERSAL DAY]
1. Scan recent higher-timeframe data (last 100+ candles, or 4H/D1 if available) for supply/demand zones — small consolidations immediately followed by a strong directional move.
2. Compute a session volume profile: POC, VAH, VAL.
3. When price enters a zone aligned with VAH or VAL, check for an absorption candle: high relative volume/delta but small net price progress (stall/rejection).
4. Signal = reversal AGAINST the current short-term direction, in the direction implied by the zone (demand zone + absorption = BUY, supply zone + absorption = SELL).

[FRIDAY = 4 — FAKEOUT DAY]
1. For any recent break of a swing high/low/liquidity pool, compute the volume/delta that accompanied the move vs. the price distance covered.
2. REAL MOVE = break + volume/delta expansion in the same direction + 1-2 candle follow-through. Signal WITH the break direction.
3. FAKEOUT = sharp wick through the level + weak/divergent volume/delta + immediate reversal candle. Signal AGAINST the spike direction (fade the trap).
4. Require higher confidence (raise internal threshold ~15% vs other days) — Friday should output fewer, cleaner signals.
