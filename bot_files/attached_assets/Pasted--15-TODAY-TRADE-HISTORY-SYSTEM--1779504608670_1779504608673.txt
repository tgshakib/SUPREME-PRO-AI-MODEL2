====================================================
15) TODAY TRADE HISTORY SYSTEM
====================================================

Add a “TODAY TRADE HISTORY” module inside AUTO TRADING DASHBOARD.

Trigger:
After every trade ends:
- Stop Loss hit
- Take Profit hit
- Closed manually
- Bot closed trade
- Timeout close
- Broker close confirm

Then auto-save trade to TODAY HISTORY.

====================================================
A) FOREX TODAY TRADE HISTORY
====================================================

Show simple compact card list.

Format:

📈 FOREX TRADE HISTORY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

EURUSD | 0.05 | +$12 🟢 PROFIT
GBPJPY | 0.10 | -$00 🔴 LOSS
XAUUSD | 0.02 | +$25 🟢 PROFIT
USDJPY | 0.03 | +$09 🟢 PROFIT
AUDUSD | 0.08 | -$00 🔴 LOSS

Each row stores:
- Pair
- Lot Size
- Entry Price
- Exit Price
- SL
- TP
- Direction (BUY/SELL)
- Profit/Loss
- Close reason
   - SL HIT
   - TP HIT
   - MANUAL CLOSE
   - BOT CLOSE
   - TIMEOUT
- Open time
- Close time
- Status
- Trade ID

Color badges:
🟢 PROFIT
🔴 LOSS
🟡 BREAK-EVEN
